About TF2 Xbox Modding

As Team Fortress 2 on the Xbox 360 moved on, Valve couldn't make anymore updates due to DLC limitations.

In 2023–2024, the modding community experienced a resurgence, led by creators like Tallpainter6446, Cosmos LLC, and others.

Cosmos is especially unique. They are reverse engineers pushing what's possible on the platform, bringing incredible custom features to the game:

  • Airblast
  • Roll The Dice
  • KillRewards & Killstreaks
  • Instant Respawn
  • Faster Build Times & Faster Teleporters
  • No Weapon Spread
  • Use Enemy Teleporters as Spy
  • Infection Menu and more!

Though momentum slowed down into 2025–2026, the scene remains active, and it's completely fine if you still just want to play vanilla.
